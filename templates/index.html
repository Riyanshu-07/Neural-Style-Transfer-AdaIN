THE ENGINE

What's happening underneath?

A quick look at what powers this neural style transfer demo.

Is this using a pretrained model?

Yes. The application uses pretrained VGG-19 features together with a pretrained AdaIN decoder for image-to-image style transfer.

What is AdaIN?

Adaptive Instance Normalization aligns the channel-wise feature statistics of the content representation with those of the style representation before decoding the result.

What kind of style images can I use?

You can experiment with paintings, sketches, illustrations, abstract artwork, textures and many other visually distinctive images.

What technology powers the application?

The project uses Python, Flask and PyTorch, with a VGG encoder, AdaIN feature transformation and a pretrained decoder.
